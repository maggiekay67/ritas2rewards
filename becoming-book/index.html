There are women who bloom quietly, and women who split the earth open just by learning themselves.

This book is for the second kind.

The collection

Born from heartbreak, rebirth, and the strange beauty of being undone.

Becoming is a collection of poems, ramblings, and psychological paradoxes — written across the years it took to be taken apart and put back together.

Nothing here is tidy. It was never supposed to be.

  • Heartbreak
  • Rebirth
  • Desire
  • Motherhood
  • Loneliness
  • Fire

What these pages hold

  • The softness you tried to hide.
  • The rage you were told to tame.
  • The love that nearly wrecked you.
  • And the woman you became anyway.

Who it's for

  • The girl who dissolved into soup.
  • The mother who gave.
  • The lover who stayed too long.
  • The woman who rose from her own ash.

And the one who finally said: enough.
